Trekking Information

Learn more about Nepal's famous trekking routes.

Everest Base Camp Trek

The Everest Base Camp trek is one of Nepal's most iconic trekking adventures. It takes travelers through the Khumbu region, where they can experience breathtaking mountain views, Sherpa hospitality, colorful villages, and high-altitude landscapes.

The journey usually begins with a flight to Lukla and continues through places such as Namche Bazaar, Tengboche, Dingboche, and Gorak Shep.

  • Best season: Spring and Autumn
  • Difficulty: Challenging
  • Highlights: Everest, Kala Patthar, Namche Bazaar

Annapurna Base Camp Trek

Annapurna Base Camp offers a spectacular journey surrounded by towering Himalayan peaks. The trek passes through lush forests, terraced farmland, suspension bridges, and traditional Gurung villages.

Trekkers reach the Annapurna Sanctuary, a natural mountain amphitheater surrounded by incredible Himalayan scenery.

  • Best season: Spring and Autumn
  • Difficulty: Moderate to challenging
  • Highlights: Annapurna Sanctuary, Machhapuchhre

Mardi Himal Trek

Mardi Himal is a wonderful short trek for travelers who want to enjoy mountain scenery without spending weeks on the trail. It passes through beautiful rhododendron forests and peaceful mountain paths.

The viewpoint and high camp offer amazing views of Machhapuchhre, Annapurna South, and the surrounding Himalayan peaks.

  • Best season: Spring and Autumn
  • Difficulty: Moderate
  • Highlights: Forest trails, High Camp, mountain viewpoints

Langtang Valley Trek

Langtang Valley is known for its natural beauty, mountain scenery, and cultural experiences. Trekkers travel through forests, traditional Tamang settlements, and beautiful alpine valleys.

The region is a great destination for travelers looking for a Himalayan experience that combines scenic trekking with local culture.

  • Best season: Spring and Autumn
  • Difficulty: Moderate
  • Highlights: Kyanjin Gompa, glaciers, mountain views

Manaslu Circuit Trek

The Manaslu Circuit is an adventurous trekking route around Mount Manaslu. It takes travelers through remote villages, mountain valleys, Buddhist culture, and high Himalayan passes.

Crossing the Larkya La Pass is one of the most memorable experiences of the trek, offering incredible views of the surrounding mountains.

  • Best season: Spring and Autumn
  • Difficulty: Challenging
  • Highlights: Larkya La Pass, Mount Manaslu, remote villages

Upper Mustang Trek

Upper Mustang is famous for its desert-like landscapes, colorful rock formations, ancient Tibetan-influenced culture, and traditional settlements.

The trek offers a different experience from Nepal's green forests and snowy mountain trails. Travelers can explore the historic walled city of Lo Manthang and ancient monasteries.

  • Best season: Spring, Autumn, and Summer
  • Difficulty: Moderate
  • Highlights: Lo Manthang, caves, ancient monasteries

Essential Trekking Tips

Pack Properly

Carry comfortable trekking boots, warm clothing, a rain jacket, water bottles, a first aid kit, and other essential trekking equipment.

Stay Safe

Walk at a comfortable pace, stay hydrated, take rest days when needed, and pay attention to altitude sickness symptoms.

Respect Local Culture

Respect local traditions, protect nature, avoid littering, and support local communities during your trekking journey.
